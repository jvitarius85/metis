Engagement module

Newsletter

Create email campaigns, maintain audiences, and understand delivery and engagement from a single communication workspace.

What it helps your team do

  • Build campaigns with a shared editor and newsletter theme settings.
  • Manage lists, subscribers, templates, revisions, and suppressions.
  • Keep public signup lists separate from private internal audiences.
  • Review delivery health, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and recipient activity.

Good to know

Newsletter list status and public visibility are managed independently, so organizations can support both public signup and internal-only audiences.
