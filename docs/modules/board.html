Board

Plan meetings, record decisions, and keep board work organized in a dedicated governance workspace.

What it helps your team do

  • Plan and manage board meetings, agendas, attendance, and supporting documents.
  • Record decisions, action items, committees, and announcements.
  • Use templates and compliance records to support repeatable governance work.
  • Connect relevant calendar, files, and people context.

Good to know

Board is designed for internal governance coordination, with a focused meeting detail experience for the work around each meeting.
