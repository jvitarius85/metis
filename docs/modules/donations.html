Engagement module

Donations

Track donors, gifts, deposits, campaigns, and fundraising activity from one operational workspace.

What it helps your team do

  • Maintain donor records and their giving history.
  • Record transactions, batches, and deposits.
  • Organize campaigns and review campaign performance.
  • Use reports and audit records for day-to-day fundraising administration.

Good to know

Donations shares contact context with the rest of the platform, reducing duplicate relationship records.
