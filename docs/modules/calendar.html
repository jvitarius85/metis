Operations module

Calendar

Plan shared dates and events in a central calendar designed for organizational scheduling.

What it helps your team do

  • Schedule and review shared events from a calendar dashboard.
  • Keep event planning in the same system as the people and work it supports.
  • Maintain calendar synchronization state where integrations are configured.

Good to know

Calendar is for shared organizational scheduling. Connected calendar services are configured through Settings.
