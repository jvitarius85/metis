Foundation module

Portal

The shared starting point for daily work in Metis, bringing common dashboards and workspace navigation into one place.

What it helps your team do

  • Open the platform dashboard for a shared operational view.
  • Move quickly between the tools your organization uses.
  • Review email-usage information from a common workspace.

Good to know

Portal is a required foundation module. It provides core platform access and cannot be disabled.
