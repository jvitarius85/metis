Settings

  • Set up email, payments, Google Workspace, calendar, and drive connections.
  • Manage developer settings, logging, cache, jobs, system health, and security.
  • Access backup, recovery, support, and platform-administration tools.

Good to know

Settings is required and is organized around administrator tasks while preserving stable administrative routes.
