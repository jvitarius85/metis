Engagement module

Forms

Build structured forms, share them with the right audience, and review submissions in Metis.

What it helps your team do

  • Create and edit forms through a guided builder.
  • Publish forms and review their entries and versions.
  • Use structured form data in connected contacts, campaigns, calendar, and ticketing workflows.
  • Optionally restrict delivery-area submissions using a five-digit ZIP allowlist, without location tracking.

Good to know

When delivery-area restrictions are used, Forms validates an eligible ZIP in the browser and on the server before creating submissions or payment work.
