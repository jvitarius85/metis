Engagement module

Media

Use a central media library for uploads that support pages, forms, and other connected content.

What it helps your team do

  • Browse and manage media from a dedicated library.
  • Keep uploads available to builders and connected content modules.
  • Reduce duplicate files and disconnected publishing assets.

Good to know

Media is intentionally focused on shared asset management; content modules use the library where they need images and other uploads.
