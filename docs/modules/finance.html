Operations module

Finance

Run core finance workflows with account, ledger, reconciliation, budgeting, invoicing, reporting, and Stripe-clearing tools.

What it helps your team do

  • Maintain a finance snapshot and general-ledger entries.
  • Reconcile bank activity and review matching work.
  • Plan budgets, manage invoices, and request reports.
  • Manage fiscal settings and Stripe-clearing activity.

Good to know

Finance is built around structured records and queued report requests so routine administration stays responsive as data grows.
