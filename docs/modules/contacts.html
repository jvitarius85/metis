Operations module

Contacts

Keep relationship records organized so staff have useful, communication-ready context when they need it.

What it helps your team do

  • View, organize, and update shared contact records.
  • Keep contact details and notes with the relationship they describe.
  • Give connected workflows a consistent source of contact context.

Good to know

Contacts complements People: it focuses on shared relationship records, while People manages platform users, roles, and access.
